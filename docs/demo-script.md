# DreamReel — Demo Video Script (3 minutes max)

> **Required for submission** (MiniMax Week 2026 §3 minutes max).
> Record: <https://dreamreel.app> (or localhost:3000).
> Output: 1920x1080, 30fps, MP4, H.264.
> Upload to: YouTube (unlisted) or X (the submission form asks for a public link).
> Tag @gmi_cloud and @MiniMaxAI on X.

---

## Shot list

Total: 3:00.

### 0:00–0:15 — The Hook (15s)

**Visual**: black screen → white text fades in: "Every morning you forget 4-5 dreams." → next line: "What if one of them became a film?"

**Voiceover (cold, low)**: "Every morning you forget four to five dreams. They vanish like they were never there. We think that’s a waste."

**Cut to**: DreamReel logo (slow fade-in over dark grain). Tagline underneath: "Tell me your dream. We'll shoot it for you."

**Music**: low A440 sine drone, slow tremolo, ~5s fade-in.

---

### 0:15–0:40 — The Pitch (25s)

**Visual**: B-roll of the web app — slow pan across the landing page, the breathing mic button.

**Voiceover**: "DreamReel turns a sixty-second voice description of a dream into a thirty-second AI film. Four scenes, an original score, a voiceover. Made in ninety seconds."

**On-screen text** (type-on): "4 MiniMax models · 1 web app · 90 seconds"

**Cut to**: cursor moving to the mic button.

---

### 0:40–1:20 — Live Demo: Record (40s)

**Action**: hold the mic button. Speak a real dream into it.

> "I was in this library, but the whole thing was upside down. I was flying between the shelves. The staircase was made of water. There was a woman in white who knew my name but wouldn’t say it."

**On-screen**: live waveform visualizes, countdown shows 0:60 → 0:00, then a soft click.

**Transition**: the 4-stage progress UI appears. Stage 1 lights up: "Writing the screenplay\u2026"

---

### 1:20–2:20 — Live Demo: Generation (60s)

**Action**: the 4-stage progress UI animates through its 8 stages in real-time:
- Writing the screenplay\u2026
- Shooting scene 1 of 4\u2026
- Shooting scene 2 of 4\u2026
- Shooting scene 3 of 4\u2026
- Shooting scene 4 of 4\u2026
- Scoring the music\u2026
- Recording the voiceover\u2026
- Assembling the final cut\u2026

**Speed ramp**: do this in real time on first run; in the edit, cut to a 2x time-lapse version with the 4 models' names appearing as small lower-third captions:

- "M3" → screenplay
- "H3" → video
- "Music 3.0" → score
- "Speech 2.8" → narration

**End**: progress hits 100%, video begins autoplaying.

---

### 2:20–2:50 — The Result (30s)

**Action**: the final 30-second film plays full-bleed (16:9, no UI chrome).

**On-screen** (small, lower-left, while the film plays): the model-by-model call log:
- "M3: screenplay generated"
- "H3: 4 scenes rendered"
- "Music 3.0: 30s score composed"
- "Speech 2.8: 30s narration"
- "FFmpeg: final cut assembled"

---

### 2:50–3:00 — The Close (10s)

**Visual**: zoom out from the film → the full app UI reappears → user clicks "Save to my dreams" → "My dreams" page shows the new dream as a card with thumbnail.

**Voiceover**: "Sora lets you see the world. DreamReel lets you see your own unconscious."

**End card**: "dreamreel.app · Built for MiniMax Week 2026"

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Use real Chrome, not a headless browser
- [ ] Clear cache before recording (the breathing animation should be at the start)
- [ ] Speak the dream with a real, slightly tired voice. The dream is real, the recording is real.
- [ ] The final 30-second film must be a real generated video, not a mock
- [ ] No Loom / no "this is a placeholder"
- [ ] Submit URL is reachable without login
- [ ] README in the public repo links to the same demo video
- [ ] X post: tag @gmi_cloud and @MiniMaxAI, include the demo URL

## Editing notes

- B-roll can be created by screen-recording the app and cutting
- If the 90s generation feels long in the edit, fast-forward to the result with a subtle "time-lapse" text caption
- Music bed: 80-100bpm, melancholic, low strings. Royalty-free (Epidemic Sound / Artlist)
- Keep the "Sora lets you see the world" line as the only spoken closer. The rest of the video speaks for itself.

## Variants (optional)

- 30-second cut for X: just hook + live record + final film + close
- 60-second cut for the README: skip the 4-stage progress, jump to result
- 10-second cut for the contest form: a single dream from record to film, no narration
